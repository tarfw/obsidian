IMPLEMENT IN LAYERS

The model stays small.
Reliability grows with use.

Every reliability mechanism answers a real failure: duplicate requests, stale writes, crashes, missed wake-ups, or unknown provider outcomes.

01Foundation

Records, Actions, Gateway, atomic save, Events, versions, permissions, tenant boundary, and idempotency.

02First durable flow

Runs, queue wake-ups, recovery, one approval path, and one External Effect proven through the food-order flow.

03Expand after proof

General Flow editing, broad projections, and additional workflow adapters only when real products require them.
